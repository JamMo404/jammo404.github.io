JAM MO

Multidisciplinary Artist Audio-Visual Artist 3D Artist Multimedia Director Interactive Developer
Jam Mo is a multidisciplinary artist working across CGI, real-time visuals, sound art, and interactive systems. His work explores computational perception and perceptual space through nonlinear audiovisual structures. He develops immersive systems that translate real-world data into experiential environments, focusing on spatial cognition, sensory distortion, and temporal displacement.
Jam Mo是一位多学科艺术创作者，专注于CGI、实时视觉、声音艺术与交互系统的融合。他的创作围绕“计算感知”与“知觉空间”，通过非线性视听结构构建沉浸式体验，将现实数据转译为感知环境，探索空间认知、感官错位与时间重构。
