Jam Mo is a multidisciplinary artist whose practice focuses on CGI animation, real-time visuals, sound art, and interactive systems. His work explores computational perception and perceptual space through nonlinear audiovisual structures, integrating technology, culture, and sensory experience across multiple domains including performance, installations, and experimental media environments.
Jam Mo是一位多学科艺术创作者，其实践聚焦于CGI动画、实时视觉、声音艺术与交互系统。作品围绕“计算感知”与“知觉空间”展开，通过非线性视听结构融合技术、文化与感知经验，广泛应用于舞台、装置与实验媒体环境。
