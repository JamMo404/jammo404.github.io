Jam Mo is a multidisciplinary artist whose practice focuses on the intersection of CGI animation, programmable real-time visuals, sound art, and interactive installations. His work engages in multi-dimensional explorations of technology, the sense of the supernatural, and non-linear spatial narratives. Grounded in a conceptual framework of computational perception and perceptual space, his practice investigates how multiple modes of perception produce experiences of temporal and spatial dislocation.
